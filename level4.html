Hide the N

Focus: Ihfa (hide the nun sakin or tanwin into a soft nasal hum that glides toward the next letter)..
⚠️ Watch out: English and Turkish habits push a crisp, hard N. Instead soften and hide it into a hum that glides to the next letter, as in el-insâne (Al-Asr) and min şerri (Al-Falaq, four times). Purple marks are ihfa. Genuine nun-ihfa count: 1 in Al-Asr, 1 in Quraysh (min cû'in), 1 in Al-Fil (min siccîl only, the tarmîhim bi spot is a mim rule, not this one), and 4 in Al-Falaq. Al-Falaq also carries strong qalqala stops (felak, halak, vekab, el-ukad, hased), so keep those blue bounces clean too.

Tajweed color key
👃 Ghunnah / idgham with hum
Nasal hum about 2 counts, including tanwin that merges and hums.
🫥 Ikhfa
Hide the silent n/tanwin into a soft nasal hum.
🔔 Qalqalah
Light bounce on silent q t b j d.
↔️ Madd
Stretch the long vowel, 2 to 6 counts.
🌑 Silent / sun-lam
Written but not pronounced, or glided.
🔁 Shadda
Double the letter (hold and stress).
