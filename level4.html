Class cue: 10 min teach · 12 min practice · 3 min close

Today you learn ihfa: hide the N.

You still write noon or tanwin.

Success: no hard English N. Soft hum into the next sound.

Ihfa (hide the N)

Ihfa means hide.

When noon sakin or tanwin comes before certain letters, hide the N.

Do not say a crisp N. Soften it. Glide toward the next letter with a light nose sound.

If a letter has no fatha, kasra, damma, and no shadda, it is sukoon.

Sukoon means that letter has no vowel. Read it closed.

Example idea: noon before dhal is ihfa, not shadda. There is only one noon.

Finger tip: start the N, then melt it before your tongue fully parks.

Watch out

English and Turkish habits push a crisp, hard N.

Instead soften and hide it into a hum that glides to the next letter, as in el-insâne (Al-Asr) and min şerri (Al-Falaq, four times).

Purple marks are ihfa.

Genuine nun-ihfa count: 1 in Al-Asr, 1 in Quraysh (min cû'in), 1 in Al-Fil (min siccîl only, the tarmîhim bi spot is a mim rule, not this one), and 4 in Al-Falaq.

Al-Falaq also carries strong qalqala stops (felak, halak, vekab, el-ukad, hased), so keep those blue bounces clean too.

FAQ: weird mushaf marks
What if a letter has no mark on it?

If there is no fatha, kasra, or damma, and no shadda, it is sukoon.

Sukoon means that letter has no vowel.

Read it closed, then go to the next letter.

Some fonts make the little sukoon circle hard to see. The rule still holds.

What is the little w shape ّ ?

That is shadda.

Say the letter strongly, like it is doubled.

Often you also hum if it is noon or meem.

What is the weird little mark on things like مٍۢ ?

That small high meem mark means iqlab.

When noon or tanwin comes before ba, turn the N into a soft M sound.

Hum a little. Do not say a hard N.

What does لا above the line mean?

Do not stop here.

Keep reading through that spot.

What does a small م pause mark mean?

You must stop here.

Finish the word, then pause.

What does ج mean for pausing?

You may stop, or you may continue.

Both are allowed.

Why are some letters gray or silent on this site?

Some marks are written but not voiced in that spot.

Follow the colored reading and the Latin under the word.

Ask your teacher if a letter still looks confusing.
