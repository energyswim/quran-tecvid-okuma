Give It a Bounce

Focus: Qalqala (the springy, vowel-less bounce on the letters qaf, ta, ba, jim, dal)..
Class cue: 10 min teach · 12 min practice · 3 min close

Today you learn qalqala: the bounce.

Some letters bounce when they have no vowel.

The bounce letters are qaf, ta, ba, jim, and dal.

Success: a clean spring sound with no extra vowel stuck on.

Qalqala (give it a bounce)

Qalqala is a springy bounce on qaf, ta, ba, jim, or dal when that letter has sukoon or you stop on it.

Bounce the letter. Do not add a vowel after it.

Wrong: a-HA-du. Right: a clean a-HAD bounce.

Stronger bounces often happen at the end of an ayah.

Finger tip: feel a tiny echo in your mouth, then stop clean.

Watch out

Kids add a vowel to the bounced letter, saying a-HA-du instead of a clean springy a-HAD.

Bounce the stop letter with no vowel attached.

Blue marks are qalqala.

Note the lighter mid-word cases: the ba in al-ebter (Kawthar) and yalid in Al-Ikhlas.

Strong ayah-end bounces: ehad, samad, yûled, and the final ehad.

An-Nasr has two lighter bounces: the dal of yedhulûne and the ta of el-feth.

Al-Ikhlas also shows one no-hum merge (lem yekün lehû) as pre-exposure only.

That rule is taught for real in Level 5.

Tajweed color key
👃 Ghunnah / idgham with hum
Nasal hum about 2 counts, including tanwin that merges and hums.
🫥 Ikhfa
Hide the silent n/tanwin into a soft nasal hum.
🔗 Idgham (no hum)
The n vanishes with NO hum and doubles into the next letter (lam or ra).
🔔 Qalqalah
Light bounce on silent q t b j d.
↔️ Madd
Stretch the long vowel, 2 to 6 counts.
🌑 Silent / sun-lam
Written but not pronounced, or glided.
🔁 Shadda
Double the letter (hold and stress).
FAQ: weird mushaf marks
What if a letter has no mark on it?

If there is no fatha, kasra, or damma, and no shadda, it is sukoon.

Sukoon means that letter has no vowel.

Read it closed, then go to the next letter.

Some fonts make the little sukoon circle hard to see. The rule still holds.

What is the little w shape ّ ?

That is shadda.

Say the letter strongly, like it is doubled.

Often you also hum if it is noon or meem.

What is the weird little mark on things like مٍۢ ?

That small high meem mark means iqlab.

When noon or tanwin comes before ba, turn the N into a soft M sound.

Hum a little. Do not say a hard N.

What does لا above the line mean?

Do not stop here.

Keep reading through that spot.

What does a small م pause mark mean?

You must stop here.

Finish the word, then pause.

What does ج mean for pausing?

You may stop, or you may continue.

Both are allowed.

Why are some letters gray or silent on this site?

Some marks are written but not voiced in that spot.

Follow the colored reading and the Latin under the word.

Ask your teacher if a letter still looks confusing.
