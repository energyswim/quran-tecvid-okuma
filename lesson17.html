Qalqala (give it a bounce)

Qalqala is a springy bounce on qaf, ta, ba, jim, or dal when that letter has sukoon or you stop on it.

Bounce the letter. Do not add a vowel after it.

Wrong: a-HA-du. Right: a clean a-HAD bounce.

Stronger bounces often happen at the end of an ayah.

Finger tip: feel a tiny echo in your mouth, then stop clean.

Practice cue

Read Subhaneke, then check Latin once.

🤲 Subhaneke prayer

Subhaneke-Allahumme ve bi hamdike ve tebarekesmuke ve teaala jedduke ve la ilahe ghayruke.
Glory and praise to You, O Allah. Blessed is Your Name and exalted is Your Majesty. There is no god but You.

FAQ: weird mushaf marks
What if a letter has no mark on it?

If there is no fatha, kasra, or damma, and no shadda, it is sukoon.

Sukoon means that letter has no vowel.

Read it closed, then go to the next letter.

Some fonts make the little sukoon circle hard to see. The rule still holds.

What is the little w shape ّ ?

That is shadda.

Say the letter strongly, like it is doubled.

Often you also hum if it is noon or meem.

What is the weird little mark on things like مٍۢ ?

That small high meem mark means iqlab.

When noon or tanwin comes before ba, turn the N into a soft M sound.

Hum a little. Do not say a hard N.

What does لا above the line mean?

Do not stop here.

Keep reading through that spot.

What does a small م pause mark mean?

You must stop here.

Finish the word, then pause.

What does ج mean for pausing?

You may stop, or you may continue.

Both are allowed.

Why are some letters gray or silent on this site?

Some marks are written but not voiced in that spot.

Follow the colored reading and the Latin under the word.

Ask your teacher if a letter still looks confusing.
